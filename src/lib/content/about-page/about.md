Hello there!

Rhea Pradeep (that's me!) is an Indian visual storyteller and illustrator from a little bit of everywhere. Right now, she’s in Providence, Rhode Island receiving her MFA in Illustration at the Rhode Island School of Design. Rhea loves to talk about a lot of things (including the ocean, space and dinosaurs) but talking about herself is definitely not one of them - so this is pretty much it for her bio. She will never say no to a cookie or a picture of a dog. 

If you want to know more about her, you can read some of the stuff she wrote here.
